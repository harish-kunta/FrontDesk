PRODUCT: TRAILFLEX 22 DAYPACK

The TrailFlex 22 is a lightweight everyday pack for day hikes, commuting, and short trips. It has a 22-liter capacity, weighs approximately 0.92 kilograms (2.0 pounds), and measures about 48 centimeters high, 28 centimeters wide, and 18 centimeters deep. The main fabric is recycled 210-denier ripstop polyester with a water-resistant coating. The pack is not waterproof.

The main compartment includes a padded sleeve that fits most laptops up to 15 inches. Laptop dimensions vary, so a device thicker than 2.5 centimeters or wider than 36 centimeters may not fit. The sleeve is suspended slightly above the bottom of the bag but is not a hard protective case. A front organizer pocket holds small items, and two stretch side pockets fit most bottles up to 750 milliliters. Very wide insulated bottles may not fit in the side pockets.

The shoulder straps are lightly padded and adjustable. The TrailFlex 22 has a removable sternum strap but no hip belt. It is designed for a typical day load of up to 9 kilograms (20 pounds). We do not recommend carrying heavier loads for extended periods because the pack does not have a load-bearing frame. The TrailFlex 22 is available in Spruce, Slate, and Clay. Each color uses the same materials and has the same dimensions.

PRODUCT: SUMMITCARRY 38 TRAVEL PACK

The SummitCarry 38 is a travel and overnight pack with a 38-liter capacity. It weighs approximately 1.45 kilograms (3.2 pounds) and measures about 55 centimeters high, 32 centimeters wide, and 22 centimeters deep when packed normally. Its recycled 420-denier nylon shell is more abrasion resistant than the TrailFlex fabric, but the seams and zippers are not waterproof.

The pack opens like a suitcase and includes a padded laptop sleeve for most devices up to 16 inches, a top pocket for small items, and internal compression straps. The removable hip belt transfers some weight to the hips. The back panel fits most torso lengths from 43 to 53 centimeters (17 to 21 inches). Torso length is measured from the prominent vertebra at the base of the neck to the top of the hip bones, not by a person's height. If your torso is outside this range, the SummitCarry 38 may not fit comfortably.

The SummitCarry 38 fits most airline carry-on size limits when it is not overpacked, but airlines set their own rules and may require a gate check. We cannot guarantee that it will be accepted as a carry-on by every airline. The pack is available in Black Spruce and Deep Harbor. The two colors have the same features.

PRODUCT: WEEKENDER 32 DUFFEL

The Weekender 32 is a 32-liter soft-sided duffel for gym use and short trips. It weighs approximately 0.78 kilograms (1.7 pounds) and measures about 50 centimeters long, 27 centimeters high, and 24 centimeters deep when not full. It has a wide U-shaped main opening, a zippered end pocket for shoes or damp clothing, and a removable shoulder strap.

The Weekender 32 has no padded laptop compartment and no internal frame. It can be carried by the two hand straps or worn over one shoulder. The end pocket is separated from the main compartment by a fabric divider, not a waterproof liner. The duffel is made from recycled 600-denier polyester with a water-resistant finish. It should not be submerged or used to transport wet gear without a separate dry bag. It is available in Charcoal and Lake Blue.

PRODUCT: NORTHLINE 750 BOTTLE

The Northline 750 is a reusable stainless-steel bottle with a 750-milliliter capacity. It uses double-wall vacuum insulation and weighs about 360 grams when empty. Cold drinks usually stay cool for up to 24 hours, and hot drinks usually stay warm for up to 8 hours. These are typical results, not a temperature guarantee; outside temperature, starting temperature, and how often the lid is opened affect performance.

The bottle is 27 centimeters tall with the cap attached and has a 5.5-centimeter opening. The included screw cap has a carry loop. The cap is leak resistant when fully closed but is not designed for carbonated drinks or use in a pressurized container. The bottle body is dishwasher safe on the top rack; the cap should be washed by hand and air-dried with the seal removed. Do not put the bottle in a microwave or freezer. The Northline 750 is available in Stone, Pine, and Rust.

PRODUCT: RIDGELINE RAIN COVER

The Ridgeline Rain Cover is a pack cover intended for light to moderate rain. Size Small fits most 15- to 22-liter packs, including the TrailFlex 22. Size Medium fits most 23- to 35-liter packs, including the Weekender 32 when carried as a pack. Size Large fits most 36- to 50-liter packs, including the SummitCarry 38. Pack shapes differ, so a very tall or unusually wide pack may need a different size.

The elastic edge and adjustable clip help keep the cover in place. The cover is made from coated recycled polyester and weighs approximately 90 grams in Small, 115 grams in Medium, and 140 grams in Large. It packs into an attached storage pouch. The cover reduces rain exposure but does not make a pack waterproof. Water can still enter through exposed straps, seams, zippers, or the back panel. For electronics or clothing that must stay dry, place those items in a separate waterproof liner inside the pack.

PRODUCT: CAMP KITCHEN ROLL

The Camp Kitchen Roll is a compact organizer for utensils and small cooking tools. It measures 42 centimeters by 28 centimeters when open and rolls to about 28 centimeters by 9 centimeters. It has six fabric sleeves, a zippered pocket, and a snap closure. The roll weighs approximately 240 grams and is made from recycled 600-denier polyester.

The Camp Kitchen Roll does not include cookware, utensils, fuel, or a cutting board. It is not heat resistant. Do not put hot pans, lit equipment, or sharp blades directly into the organizer. Clean surface dirt with a damp cloth and mild soap, then air-dry fully before storage. It is available in Moss and Graphite.

CHOOSING A PACK SIZE AND FIT

Capacity in liters describes the approximate space inside a bag; it does not mean that every item with a matching volume will fit. Long objects, rigid boxes, and bulky insulated clothing can use space inefficiently. Check the listed external measurements as well as capacity when choosing a pack.

For a typical day hike with water, lunch, a light layer, and a small first-aid kit, the TrailFlex 22 is usually sufficient. For an overnight trip with a compact sleeping bag and a change of clothes, the SummitCarry 38 offers more structure and a hip belt. For clothing and shoes on a short trip, the Weekender 32 provides a wide opening but has no frame or laptop padding.

To measure torso length, tilt your head forward and locate the prominent bone at the base of your neck. Place your hands on the top of your hip bones with your thumbs pointing toward each other. Measure the distance between the neck bone and the line between your thumbs. The SummitCarry 38 is designed for a torso length of 43 to 53 centimeters. The TrailFlex 22 has adjustable shoulder straps but does not have a torso-length adjustment system.

The dimensions in this guide are measured on an unfilled product. A bag's shape changes when it is packed. If you need to fit a particular laptop, camera case, medical device, or airline sizer, compare that item's measurements with the pack's internal and external measurements before ordering. Contact support if you need help comparing dimensions.

WATER RESISTANCE AND WEATHER CARE

Water-resistant fabric can shed light rain for a limited time. It does not mean a product is waterproof. Rain can enter through seams, zippers, openings, and back panels, especially during prolonged rain or when a bag is set on wet ground. The TrailFlex 22, SummitCarry 38, Weekender 32, and Ridgeline Rain Cover are water resistant, not waterproof.

For heavy rain, use the Ridgeline Rain Cover over a pack and put important items in an internal waterproof liner. Dry a wet bag at room temperature with all compartments open. Do not place it on a radiator, use a tumble dryer, or store it while damp. Heat can damage coatings and adhesives, and damp storage can lead to mildew.

The fabric coating gradually wears with use, abrasion, and cleaning. We do not recommend applying wax or a household waterproofing spray without checking that the product is safe for recycled polyester or nylon. A treatment may change fabric color or affect the warranty. Ask support before applying a chemical treatment if you are unsure.

WARRANTY AND REPAIRS

Harbor & Pine products include a one-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty period begins on the delivery date and applies to the original purchaser with proof of purchase. It does not cover ordinary wear, cosmetic marks, accidental damage, misuse, overloading, unauthorized repairs, modifications, or damage caused by improper cleaning or storage.

Examples that may be reviewed as a manufacturing defect include a shoulder strap that separates at a seam during normal use, a zipper slider that fails shortly after purchase, or a buckle that arrives cracked. Examples that are not usually covered include abrasion from rocks, a cut from a sharp object, fabric damage from overloading, discoloration from bleach, and damage caused by leaving a wet pack in a closed car.

To request warranty help, email help@harborandpine.example with the order number, product name, delivery date, and photos showing the issue. Do not mail the product before support provides instructions. If a claim is approved, we may repair the product, replace it with the same item, offer a comparable item, or refund the purchase price. The remedy depends on the product and parts available. The warranty does not cover shipping for issues outside the United States.

We do not currently operate a general repair service and cannot promise replacement buckles, straps, or zippers for every product. Support can suggest a local gear-repair shop for damage outside the warranty. A third-party repair does not automatically void coverage for unrelated manufacturing defects, but damage caused by the repair is not covered.

PRODUCT CARE AND CLEANING

For packs and duffels, empty all pockets and shake out loose dirt. Spot-clean with lukewarm water, a soft cloth, and a small amount of mild soap. Rinse the cloth and wipe away soap residue. Air-dry completely with zippers and compartments open. Do not machine wash, bleach, dry-clean, iron, or tumble-dry a pack or duffel.

For the Northline 750 bottle, wash the stainless-steel body on the top rack of a dishwasher or by hand with warm soapy water. Hand-wash the cap. Remove the silicone seal periodically, wash underneath it, and let all parts dry fully before reassembly. Do not store milk or sugary drinks in the bottle for an extended period. If an odor remains, soak the body with warm water and baking soda, then rinse thoroughly.

For the Ridgeline Rain Cover, wipe off dirt with a damp cloth and mild soap. Let it dry fully before folding it into its attached pouch. For the Camp Kitchen Roll, wipe the fabric and air-dry it fully. Do not expose the organizer to an open flame or hot cookware.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Question: Is the TrailFlex 22 waterproof?
Answer: No. It has water-resistant fabric, but seams, zippers, and openings can let water in. Use a rain cover and an internal liner for important items.

Question: Will the TrailFlex 22 hold a 16-inch laptop?
Answer: The padded sleeve is designed for most laptops up to 15 inches. A 16-inch laptop is not guaranteed to fit. Compare the laptop's dimensions with the sleeve limit of about 36 centimeters wide and 2.5 centimeters thick.

Question: Does the SummitCarry 38 fit every airline carry-on rule?
Answer: No. It fits most common carry-on limits when not overpacked, but airlines set their own size rules and may require a gate check.

Question: Can I wash the Weekender 32 in a washing machine?
Answer: No. Spot-clean it with lukewarm water and mild soap, then air-dry completely.

Question: How much does standard shipping cost?
Answer: Standard shipping is $6.95 for U.S. orders under $75 before tax and after discounts. It is free for eligible orders of $75 or more.

Question: How long do I have to return an unused pack?

Question: Is there a fee to return an item because I changed my mind?

Question: What should I do if the bottle cap leaks?
Answer: Confirm that the cap is fully closed and the silicone seal is clean and seated correctly. The cap is leak resistant, not intended for carbonated drinks or pressurized use. If it appears defective within one year, contact support with photos and the order number.

Question: Do you ship to Canada?
Answer: No. Harbor & Pine currently ships to the 50 U.S. states and Washington, D.C., but not to international addresses, U.S. territories, APO/FPO addresses, or freight-forwarding warehouses.

Question: Can I pick up my online order at the Duluth mailing address?

Question: What is the warranty period?
Answer: Products have a one-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship. It does not cover ordinary wear, accidental damage, misuse, or unauthorized repairs.

Question: Does the rain cover make my backpack waterproof?
Answer: No. It reduces rain exposure, but water can still enter through openings and exposed areas. Put important items in an internal waterproof liner.

Question: Can support change the shipping address after an order ships?
Answer: No. We can only request order changes before warehouse processing starts. Once the carrier has the package, the address cannot be changed by Harbor & Pine.

Question: What if my tracking says delivered but I do not see the package?
Answer: Check nearby delivery areas and ask household members or building staff. Wait one full business day after the delivery scan. If it is still missing, contact support so we can open a carrier inquiry.
